Your phone. Your data.

Privacy isn't an extra feature.

MercuReal Data Guard is built to work locally. It doesn't need an account, cloud service or internet connection to show your usage.

What stays on your device

Usage settings, cost preferences and bundle details are stored locally. The app has no INTERNET permission, backend, analytics or cloud backup.

Honest limits: costs are estimates from your price, not operator billing. Bundle remaining is device traffic since you added it, not your carrier balance. Device and OEM support can affect usage reporting; not yet validated on a physical device.
