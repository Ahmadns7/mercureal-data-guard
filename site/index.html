What it does

Simple, honest data awareness.

Per-app usage

See today's and this month's mobile data by app using Android's NetworkStats.

Naira cost estimate

Set your own price per GB and optional monthly budget. Know what you spend.

Bundle tracker

Add bundles with size, price and validity. See time left and expiring-soon warnings.

Fully local

Data stays on your phone. No internet permission, backend, analytics or cloud backup.

Privacy by design

The app cannot send your data anywhere because it has no INTERNET permission.

Honest limits: costs are estimates from your own price, not operator billing. Bundle "remaining" is device traffic since you added the bundle, not your carrier's balance. Not yet validated on a physical device.

Build it yourself

git clone https://github.com/Ahmadns7/mercureal-data-guard
cd mercureal-data-guard
gradle :app:testDebugUnitTest :app:assembleDebug
